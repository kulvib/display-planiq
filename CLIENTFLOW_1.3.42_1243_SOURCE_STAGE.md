## Incident-driven purpose and physical baseline

On **2026-11-06**, the *Viborg2* test kiosk (**backend client ID 2**) started an authorized 1.3.38/1239 → 1.3.41/1242 update. Artifact verification and staging passed, but activation and automatic rollback failed, leaving the runtime stopped and a durable `activation_intent`. A controlled recovery subsequently restored **1.3.38/1239** with `rollback_health_verified`, `automatic_rollback_completed` and `activation_intent=None`; the superadmin later acknowledged local recovery. The 1.3.41/1242 artifact and its failure evidence are immutable.

The physical repair on Viborg2 was an *attended*, 1.3.38-only controller bridge, verified with `CLIENTFLOW_1338_CONTROLLER_BRIDGE=PASS`. The controller now uses `ProtectHome=tmpfs` and `BindPaths=/home/clientflow-kiosk` rather than the old loaded `ProtectHome=yes` sandbox. The `CLIENTFLOW_CONTROLLER_SANDBOX_PROBE=PASS` and independent home-namespace, kiosk ACL dry-run, unrelated-home isolation and critical-service checks also passed. This is evidence that the previous permission barrier is repaired **on this device**, not evidence that automatic rollback or a full 1.3.42 update has passed.
